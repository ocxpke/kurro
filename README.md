# Kurro

> Connecting local artisans and service providers with the businesses that need them — with verified, two-way reviews.

Built for the **MarbellaUp Hackathon** (Marbella, Andalucía).

---

## What is Kurro?

Kurro is a platform that connects **small artisans and local service providers** with **businesses** such as hotels, restaurants and event organisers.

Local professionals get a portfolio-style profile where they show their work, their availability and their reviews. Businesses can find trusted professionals nearby in minutes, including people available **the same day** for temporary or urgent jobs.

Reputation is built through a **bidirectional, verified and anonymous review system**: workers review employers, and employers review workers. This rewards quality work and good working environments, and makes the local economy more efficient.

## The problem

- Small artisans and local workers have no easy, affordable way to reach business clients without expensive intermediaries.
- Businesses with high staff turnover (hospitality, tourism, events) struggle to find reliable, available local professionals quickly.
- Generic job platforms (LinkedIn, Glassdoor, InfoJobs) are not built for short-term local work, do not showcase what a professional actually makes, and do not offer reviews in both directions.

## Key features

- **Portfolio profiles**: showcase work, products, availability and reviews.
- **Urgency filter**: see which professionals are available today, ideal for temporary jobs.
- **Two-way reviews**: verified and anonymous reviews of the service *and* of the employer.
- **Local focus**: proximity providers for businesses in the area.
- **Marketplace and hiring in one app**: hire services and sell or buy products between businesses, with sales protection.
- **Badges and levels**: "Verified professional", "Recommended employer", "Fast response".
- **Favourites and one-click rehire** for businesses.
- **Freemium accounts**, with premium plans for high-demand businesses.

### Ideas on the roadmap

- Smart alerts: businesses define filters (cost, service type, dates, duration) and are notified when a matching offer appears.
- Swipe-style discovery of offers and professionals.
- In-app payments with a platform commission.

## Who is it for?

| User | What they get |
|------|---------------|
| **Local professionals and artisans** | Show their work, win clients without costly intermediaries and build a reputation that belongs to them. |
| **Businesses with high demand for workers** | Find a trusted local professional in minutes, backed by real reviews. |
| **Businesses that sell or buy products** | Reach other businesses through the app, with sales protection. |
| **People actively looking for work** | Find short-term and local opportunities. |

## How Kurro is different

- Combines a **sales platform** and a **hiring platform** in one product.
- **Bidirectional reviews** that benefit both workers and employers.
- Reputation is **not portable** to other platforms, so it gives users a natural reason to stay.
- Focus on **proximity** and **immediate availability**.

## Business model

- Premium plans for businesses with high worker turnover and urgent needs.
- Premium plans for businesses selling products through the app, with sales protection.
- Advertising.
- Potential commission on in-app payments.
